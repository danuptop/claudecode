## TWEET 15 — The Gap Question is Coming

Took 8 months off?

AI flagged it.
The interviewer will ask about it.

Have a one-sentence answer ready.

Not a 5-minute monologue.
Not a defensive explanation.
One clear sentence.

"I took time off to [reason],
and I came back sharper because [result]."

Rehearse it until it's boring to say.
That's how you know it's ready.
